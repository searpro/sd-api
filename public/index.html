Add component

          

            Each model is a bundle with its own checkpoint, vae and
            clip (text-encoder) files. Download into a new or existing model.
          

          

            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          

          

            Split models (e.g. Z-Image Turbo) are auto-detected: a bundle with a vae/clip is
            loaded via --diffusion-model with its VAE and text encoder wired in.
            Component roles are inferred from filenames (clip_l, t5xxl, qwen→llm, …); drop a
            model.json in the bundle to override.

Speak

            
            
            
            
            

            

              
                Voice reference — required for cloning-only models (e.g. Chatterbox)
              
              

                
                
                
                
                
                
                

                  Some TTS models only support voice cloning/conversion, not plain text-to-speech —
                  they need a short reference WAV of the target voice (and generate better with a
                  transcript of it). Uploaded once, then reusable across requests.
